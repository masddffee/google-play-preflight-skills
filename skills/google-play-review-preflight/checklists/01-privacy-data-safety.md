# Privacy and Data safety

These are human review prompts, not additional executable rules or guaranteed rejection criteria. Run the CLI first. Keep unsupported conclusions UNKNOWN and label manual findings separately from CLI output. Policy applicability depends on current official requirements and the actual app, market, audience and release.

Check actual data collection, sharing, retention and deletion, including third-party SDKs and AI services. Compare the public privacy policy, in-app disclosures, consent flow and Play Console Data safety answers. A URL or dependency cannot prove these are consistent. Apps with applicable in-app account creation need working in-app and web deletion request paths; verify exceptions and retained data disclosures. Do not infer missing Console forms from missing repository files.

CLI categories: GP-PRIVACY-001, GP-DATA-001, GP-ACCOUNT-001.

Sources: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en and https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en
