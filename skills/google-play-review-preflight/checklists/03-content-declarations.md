# Content and audience

These are human review prompts, not additional executable rules or guaranteed rejection criteria. Run the CLI first. Keep unsupported conclusions UNKNOWN and label manual findings separately from CLI output. Policy applicability depends on current official requirements and the actual app, market, audience and release.

Verify target audience, content rating, ads declarations and applicable Families requirements. Child appeal by itself is not a reliable automated targeting rule. If the app includes user-generated content, review moderation, reporting/blocking and enforcement flows against current requirements. Restricted content categories require their specific policies. An ads SDK alone does not prove ads are displayed.

CLI categories: GP-ADS-001, GP-CONTENT-001. UGC moderation is manual and not implemented as an automatic v1 check.
