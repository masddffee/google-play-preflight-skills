## Policy Update Tracking

Google Play policies are updated without a fixed schedule. To track changes:
- Subscribe to the [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/)
- Monitor the [Play Console release notes](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9842657)
- Watch for policy enforcement emails in your Play Console email address
